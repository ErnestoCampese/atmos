To begin, make a new empty folder on this computer — for example one called Atmos inside your Documents — and choose it below. Your browser will ask whether αtmos may see and change that folder: answer yes. Everything you make is kept there, and nothing is uploaded.

Has someone given you an αtmos folder already? Choose that one instead.

This browser can’t work with folders on your computer. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop or laptop.

Freesound is a website of sounds shared by people around the world. To search it from here you need your own free key. This is done once:

  1. Open freesound.org and create a free account.
  2. Open this page and fill in the short form — any name and description will do.
  3. Copy the long code shown under “Client secret/Api key” and paste it below.
