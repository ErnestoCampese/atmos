Compose your own atmosphere.

Layer rain, wind, voices and distant bells into a soundscape for the scene you are writing, then leave it playing while you write.

  1. 1Make a new, empty folder in the window that opens
  2. 2Choose it
  3. 3Say yes when your browser asks

αtmos needs Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop or laptop, because only they can keep your work in a folder on your computer.

Everything you make stays in that folder on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.

Already have an αtmos folder, from another computer or from someone else? Choose that one instead.

Freesound is a free website of sounds shared by people around the world. To search it from here, αtmos needs a free key of your own. Getting one takes about two minutes, and you do it once:

  1. Make a free account on freesound.org.
  2. Open Freesound’s page for keys and fill in the short form. Any name and description will do.
  3. Copy the long code under “Client secret/Api key” and paste it below.
