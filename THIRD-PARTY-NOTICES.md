### Alegreya

- Files: `assets/fonts/alegreya-roman-latin.woff2`, `alegreya-roman-latin-ext.woff2` and
  `alegreya-roman-greek.woff2` (upright, weights 400 to 500), and `alegreya-italic-latin.woff2`,
  `alegreya-italic-latin-ext.woff2` and `alegreya-italic-greek.woff2` (italic, weight 400): the
  Latin, Latin Extended and Greek subsets as served by Google Fonts. The Greek alpha is the α of
  the wordmark.
- The app icons (`assets/icons/`) and the README banner (`assets/readme-banner.svg`) draw the name
  with Alegreya italic's letters, converted to outlines.
